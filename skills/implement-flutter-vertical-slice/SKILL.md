boundaries. Match approved design while adapting interaction to Android and iOS
conventions. Validate network and persisted data, expose typed failures, handle loading,
empty, offline, error, authorization, retry, restoration, and success states, and add
unit, widget, golden, and integration coverage appropriate to the change.
